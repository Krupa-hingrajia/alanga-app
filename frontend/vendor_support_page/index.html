Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions asked by ALANGA Vendors.

Store documents (GST, PAN, Bank Details) are verified by the ALANGA Onboarding team within 24–48 business hours after submission. You will receive an in-app confirmation once verified.
Payouts for delivered orders are credited directly to your registered bank account according to our settlement cycle, after deducting platform commission and applicable taxes.
Please update the order status directly in the Vendor App or email us at info@alangashop.com immediately with the Order ID so our support team can notify the customer and handle refunds seamlessly.
For security reasons, changes to critical bank accounts or registered business emails require verification. Email info@alangashop.com from your existing registered email with supporting bank proof (cancelled cheque or bank statement).
