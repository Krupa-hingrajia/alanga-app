1. Overview

Welcome to Alanga Vendor ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our merchant partners, sellers, and vendors who use the Alanga Vendor Application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you register, log in, manage products, upload variant images, process orders, and manage store settings through our application. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

Notice for Google Play Users: Alanga Vendor is a B2B merchant management application designed to facilitate store setup, product catalogue management, variant image uploads, and order processing for authorized sellers on the Alanga platform.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information and operational data necessary to provide seamless vendor management services.

A. Personal & Account Information

  • Vendor Profile Data: Full Name, Email Address, Mobile Phone Number, Business/Store Name, Business Address.
  • Authentication Credentials: Passwords, Authentication Tokens (stored securely on device via encrypted local storage).
  • Financial & Settlement Data: Bank Account Details, UPI ID, Tax/GST Identification Numbers (used solely for processing vendor payout settlements).

B. Media, Catalog & Content Data

  • Product & Store Media: Product images, variant photos, store banners, and business verification documents uploaded by you using your device camera or media gallery.

C. Device & Technical Information

  • Device Identifiers & Logs: Device model, OS version, IP address, app performance logs, and error/crash diagnostics to maintain server stability and prevent fraud.

3. App Permissions & Access Disclosure

To function effectively on Android devices, the Alanga Vendor App requests specific runtime permissions. We only request permissions essential for features actively used by vendors:

Permission Purpose & Play Store Justification
INTERNET Access Required to sync product listings, fetch orders, send requests to Alanga APIs, and manage vendor backend data in real time.
CAMERA & MEDIA / GALLERY Required to let vendors capture and upload product photos, store logos, variant images, and business verification documents directly from their device.
ENCRYPTED STORAGE Required to safely store authentication session tokens locally using Flutter Secure Storage (KeyStore/Keychain) for automatic, secure relogin.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly for lawful business purposes related to operating the Alanga Vendor marketplace platform:

  • To create and manage your vendor account and merchant store page.
  • To upload and update product catalogs, pricing, inventory, and variant gallery images.
  • To notify you of incoming customer orders, fulfillment updates, and delivery statuses.
  • To calculate vendor payouts, process settlements, and generate revenue reports.
  • To ensure platform security, prevent fraudulent seller activity, and verify merchant credentials.
  • To respond to your support queries and system diagnostic issues.

5. Data Sharing & Security

A. Data Protection & Encryption

We take the security of your data very seriously. All network communication between the Alanga Vendor App and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS 1.3 protocol. Sensitive credentials are securely stored using device hardware KeyStore encryption.

B. Third-Party Service Sharing

We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third-party advertisers. We only share necessary data with trusted service providers under strict confidentiality agreements:

  • Banking & Payout Partners: To transfer payment settlements to your registered bank account.
  • Cloud Hosting Infrastructure: Secure servers hosting database storage and product media assets.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, court order, or governmental regulations.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion Request

We retain your personal and store data for as long as your vendor account remains active or as required by law for financial accounting and tax compliance.

Google Play User Data Rights: You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

If you wish to permanently delete your Alanga Vendor account and remove all personal information from our active databases, you can submit a deletion request through any of the following methods:

  1. In-App Option: Navigate to Settings > Store Settings > Delete Account inside the Alanga Vendor App.
  2. Email Request: Send an email from your registered vendor email address to alanga.founder@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - [Your Store Name]".
  3. Online Deletion Page: Visit our dedicated Account Deletion Request Form Page.

Account Deletion Request Form

Submitting this form will send a direct email notification to our administrative compliance team via Web3Forms.

✓ Deletion request submitted via Web3Forms! Our support team will verify your request and contact you at your email within 48 business hours.
